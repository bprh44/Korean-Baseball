# KOREAN LESSONS COMPREHENSIVE STUDY & PRESENTATION REFERENCE GUIDE

---

## 1. INFERRED FOUNDATIONS & PREREQUISITES (LESSONS 1–13 & GAPS)

### Basic Alphabet & Greetings (Chapters 2–3, Lessons 1–3)
* **Hangeul System:** Reading, writing, and combining Korean vowels and consonants into syllable blocks.
* **Basic Greetings:** 
  * 안녕하세요? (Hello)
  * 반갑습니다 (Nice to meet you)
  * 감사합니다 / 고맙습니다 (Thank you)
  * 안녕히 계세요 / 안녕히 가세요 (Goodbye)

### Identification, Objects & Locations (Lessons 4–6)
* **Self-Introduction:** 저는 N-입니다/이에요 (I am [Noun]).
* **Nationality/Occupation Inquiries:** 어느 나라 사람입니까? (Which country's person are you?).
* **Demonstratives & Objects:** 이것 / 그것 / 저것 (This item / That item / That item over there); 이것이 무엇입니까? (What is this?).
* **Location & Existence:** N-에 있다/없다 (To be / not be at N); N-에 가다/오다 (To go to / come to N).

### Daily Actions & Tenses (Lessons 7–13)
* **Present Tense:** Polite informal (`-아/어/해요`) and formal (`-ㅂ니다/습니다`).
* **Object Marker:** N-을/를 (Direct object marker).
* **Sino-Korean Numbers & Currency:** 일, 이, 삼, 사... / 원 (Korean Won); 얼마예요? (How much is it?); N 주세요 (Please give me N).
* **Past Tense:** -았/었/했어요 (Did / completed action).

## 2. LESSONS OVERVIEW & THEMATIC TOPICS

* **Lesson 14 (어서 갑시다 - Let's Hurry):** Public transportation (subway, bus, taxi), travel methods, expressing reasons, and stating obligations.
* **Lesson 16 (내일 저녁에 바쁘세요? - Are You Busy Tomorrow Evening?):** Telling time (hours & minutes), schedule states, sequential actions, making invitations, and appointments.
* **Lesson 17 (가족이 몇 명이세요? - How Many People Are in Your Family?):** Family relationships, honorifics (-님), occupations, native numbers, and counting people (명/분).
* **Lesson 21 (옷을 한 벌 사고 싶어요 - I Want to Buy an Outfit):** Shopping for clothes, expressing desires, stating abilities/capabilities, and clothing counters (벌).
* **Lesson 22 (주말에 무엇을 할 거예요? - What Are You Going to Do on the Weekend?):** Weekend plans, future intentions, background context (-ㄴ/은/는데), and volume counters (권).
* **Lesson 23 (감기에 걸렸어요 - I Caught a Cold):** Illnesses and medical symptoms, pharmacy interactions, time durations, and suggesting actions (-아/어 보다).
* **Lesson 26 (오늘은 내가 찻값을 낼게요 - Today I Will Pay for the Drinks):** Cafes and prices, volitional promises (-(으)ㄹ게요), tag questions (-지요), and exclamations (-군요).

## 3. GRAMMAR RULES & SENTENCE STRUCTURE FORMULAS

### Category A: Intentions, Plans & Desires
1. **V-(으)ㄹ 거예요 (Future Plan / Intention)**
   * *Formula:* Verb Stem + ㄹ 거예요 (vowel) / 을 거예요 (consonant)
   * *Example:* 주말에 한국 역사책을 읽을 거예요. (I am going to read a Korean history book on the weekend.)
2. **(내가/제가) V-(으)ㄹ게요 (Volitional Promise / Immediate Intention)**
   * *Formula:* Verb Stem + ㄹ게요 (vowel) / 을게요 (consonant)
   * *Example:* 오늘은 내가 찻값을 낼게요. (Today I will pay for the drinks.)
3. **V-고 싶다 (Expressing Desire)**
   * *Formula:* Verb Stem + 고 싶어요 / 고 싶습니다
   * *Example:* 옷을 한 벌 사고 싶어요. (I want to buy an outfit.)

### Category B: Conjunctions & Clause Connections
4. **S-(으)니까 S (Causal Connection / "Because...")**
   * *Formula:* Stem + 니까 (vowel) / 으니까 (consonant)
   * *Example:* 시간이 없으니까 택시를 탑시다. (Since we don't have time, let's take a taxi.)
5. **S-아서/어서 S (Sequential Actions / "And then...")**
   * *Formula:* Verb Stem conjugated with 아서 / 어서
   * *Example:* 학교에 가서 친구를 만났습니다. (I went to school and met a friend.)
6. **S-ㄴ/은/는데 S (Background Context / "..., so/and...")**
   * *Formula:* Action Verb + 는데 / Adjective + ㄴ데/은데 / Noun + 인데
   * *Example:* 어제 책을 한 권 샀는데 내일 읽으려고 해요. (I bought a book yesterday, and I plan to read it tomorrow.)

### Category C: Obligations, Capabilities & Trial Actions
7. **V-아야/어야 하다 (Obligation / "Must / Have to")**
   * *Formula:* Verb Stem + 아야 하다 (for ㅏ/ㅗ) / 어야 하다 (for other vowels)
   * *Example:* 오늘은 학교에 가야 합니다. (I have to go to school today.)
8. **V-(으)ㄹ 수 있다 / 없다 (Capability / "Can / Cannot")**
   * *Formula:* Verb Stem + ㄹ 수 있다 (vowel) / 을 수 있다 (consonant)
   * *Example:* 나는 한국말을 할 수 있습니다. (I can speak Korean.)
9. **V-아/어 보다 (Trial / "Try Doing")**
   * *Formula:* Verb Stem + 아 보다 / 어 보다
   * *Example:* 이 책을 읽어 보세요. (Try reading this book.)

### Category D: Suggestions, Proposals & Reactions
10. **V-(으)ㄹ까요? (Seeking Opinions / Suggestions)**
    * *Formula:* Verb Stem + ㄹ까요? / 을까요?
    * *Example:* 어떻게 갈까요? (How shall we go?)
11. **V-ㅂ시다 / 읍시다 (Proposals / "Let's...")**
    * *Formula:* Verb Stem + ㅂ시다 (vowel) / 읍시다 (consonant)
    * *Example:* 지하철을 탑시다. (Let's take the subway.)
12. **A/V-(이)지요? (Seeking Confirmation / "...Right?")**
    * *Formula:* Stem + 지요? (Verb/Adj) / Noun + 이지요?
    * *Example:* 철수는 학생이지요? (Chulsoo is a student, right?)
13. **A-군요 / V-는군요 (Exclamation / Realization)**
    * *Formula:* Adjective Stem + 군요 / Verb Stem + 는군요
    * *Example:* 집값이 참 비싸군요! (Housing prices are really expensive!)

### Category E: Modifiers, Identification & Possession
14. **A-ㄴ/은 N (Adjectives Modifying Nouns)**
    * *Formula:* Adjective Stem + ㄴ (vowel) / 은 (consonant) + Noun
    * *Example:* 복잡한 거리 (a crowded street) / 좋은 약 (good medicine)
15. **N-이에/예요 vs. N-(이)세요 (Identification)**
    * *Formula:* Polite Identification: N-이에요/예요 | Honorific Identification: N-(이)세요
    * *Example:* 제 친구예요. (He is my friend.) / 저분이 누구세요? (Who is that person?)
16. **N이/가 아니에요 (Negation)**
    * *Formula:* Noun + 이/가 아니에요
    * *Example:* 여가는 2층이 아니에요. (This is not the second floor.)
17. **N이/가 있다 / 없다 (Possession & Existence)**
    * *Formula:* Noun + 이/가 있다/없다
    * *Example:* 형이 하나 있어요. (I have one older brother.) / 저는 차가 없어요. (I don't have a car.)

### Category F: Time, Transportation & Location Markers
18. **N을/를 타다 / N에서 내리다 (Riding / Exiting Vehicles)**
    * *Example:* 사당역에서 전철을 탔어요. (I took the train at Sadang Station.)
19. **N부터 N까지 (Time Span / "From... To...")**
    * *Example:* 월요일부터 금요일까지 (From Monday to Friday)
20. **N 전에 (Time Prior / "Ago / Before")**
    * *Example:* 30분 전에 도착했어요. (I arrived 30 minutes ago.)
21. **N와/과 같이 / N와/과 함께 (Company / "Together with")**
    * *Example:* 친구와 같이 산에 가요. (I go to the mountain with my friend.)
22. **N에 다니다 (Attendance / Working at)**
    * *Example:* 우리 형은 회사에 다녀요. (My older brother works at a company.)

## 4. IRREGULAR VERB CONJUGATION PATTERNS

1. **ㄷ-Irregular (e.g., 걷다 - to walk, 듣다 - to listen):**
   * Stem ㄷ changes to ㄹ before a vowel.
   * *Examples:* 걷다 → 걸어요 (walks), 걸읍시다 (let's walk); 듣다 → 들어요 (listens).
2. **르-Irregular (e.g., 오르다 - to rise, 빠르다 - to be fast):**
   * Stem ㅡ drops and an extra ㄹ is added to the preceding syllable.
   * *Examples:* 오르다 → 올랐습니다 (has risen); 빠르다 → 빨라서 (because it is fast).
3. **ㅡ-Irregular (e.g., 바쁘다 - to be busy, 아프다 - to be sick):**
   * Stem ㅡ drops when combining with 아/어 endings.
   * *Examples:* 바쁘다 → 바빠요 (is busy); 아프다 → 아파요 (is sick).

## 5. ESSENTIAL VOCABULARY & COUNTER SUMMARY

* **People Counters:** 명 (standard count), 분 (honorific count)
* **Clothing Counter:** 벌 (outfits/clothes)
* **Volume Counter:** 권 (books)
* **Item & Container Counters:** 개 (general items), 병 (bottles)
* **Time System:**
  * Hours: Native Korean Numbers (한 시, 두 시, 세 시, 네 시...)
  * Minutes: Sino-Korean Numbers (일 분, 이 분, 오 분, 십 분...)
* **Key Medical/Symptoms Terms:** 감기 (cold), 열 (fever), 기침 (cough), 아프다 (to be sick/hurt), 약국 (pharmacy).
* **Key Shopping/Cafe Terms:** 옷 (clothes), 찻값 (price of tea/drinks), 내다 (to pay), 사고 싶다 (want to buy), 얼마 (how much).
